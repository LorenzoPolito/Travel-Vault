# 🏘️ Chinatown

Travolgente quartiere tra SoHo e LES. Mott Street, Doyers Street (l'angolo più storico), Columbus Park. Street food, soup dumplings da [[Locations/UnitedStates/NewYork/Ristoranti/Joe's Shanghai]] e pork bun da Mei Lai Wah.
